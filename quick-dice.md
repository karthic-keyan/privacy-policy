# Privacy Policy for Quick Dice

**Last Updated: November 1, 2025**

## Introduction

Quick Dice ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Roll History**: We store your dice roll history locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
- **User Preferences**: Settings such as number of dice, animation speed, and break time are stored locally on your device.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- We do **NOT** collect any personal information automatically.
- We do **NOT** collect location data.
- We do **NOT** use analytics or tracking services.
- We do **NOT** collect device identifiers or device information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your roll history and preferences are used solely to:
- Display your dice rolls in the History tab
- Maintain your app settings and preferences
- Provide an improved user experience

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: All your data (roll history and preferences) is stored locally on your device only.
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do not transmit, store, or backup your data to any servers.
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: Your data is never shared with third parties.

## 4. Permissions

The Quick Dice app requires the following permissions:
- **Storage**: To save your roll history locally on your device
- **No Internet**: This app does not require internet connectivity

## 5. Third-Party Services

Quick Dice does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Quick Dice is designed for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app collects no personal information whatsoever.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

- **Access**: You can access your roll history anytime in the History tab.
- **Deletion**: You can clear your history at any time using the "Clear All" button in the History tab.
- **Control**: All data storage is under your complete control.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## 9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

**Email**: support@ktechinnovation.com  
**Project**: Quick Dice - Dice Roller App

## 10. Data Deletion

To delete all your data:
1. Open the Quick Dice app
2. Go to the History tab
3. Tap the "Clear All" button
4. Confirm deletion

All your roll history and stored data will be permanently deleted from your device.
