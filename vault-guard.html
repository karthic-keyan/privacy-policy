Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • VaultGuard: Password Manager (com.vaultguard.app) • Published by k-Tech Innovation

Your vault never leaves your phone

Your vault is encrypted and stored only on your device. We have no servers, no accounts and no ads, and we can never see or recover your passwords.

1. Information you store in the App

You choose what to save. Depending on the item type, a vault item can contain:

  • Passwords: title, username or email, password, website and notes.
  • Secure notes: the note text.
  • Credit cards: card name, cardholder name, card number, expiry date, CVV, PIN, bank and notes.
  • Identities: full name, date of birth, gender, email, phone number, address, ID document number and notes.
  • Folders you create to organise items.

All of this stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. How your vault is protected

  • Encryption: item titles, item contents and folder names are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved in the App's private database. Only non-sensitive details (item type, favourite flag and created/updated times) are stored unencrypted, so the App can sort and filter.
  • Master password: your vault key is protected by a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2-SHA256 with a random salt). The master password itself is never stored or transmitted.
  • We cannot recover your vault. Because we never have your master password or your data, a forgotten master password cannot be reset by us.
  • Fingerprint unlock (optional): if you turn it on, a protected copy of the vault key is kept in Android's secure storage (Android Keystore). Fingerprint checks are done by Android; the App never receives or stores your fingerprint.
  • Auto-lock: the vault locks after the timeout you choose and, if enabled, when the App goes to the background. The decrypted key is kept only in memory while the vault is unlocked.
  • Password generator: passwords are generated on your device using the system's secure random number generator.

3. Clipboard

When you tap copy, the value is placed on the Android clipboard so you can paste it into another app. The App clears the clipboard automatically 30 seconds later. While a value is on the clipboard, Android and the app you paste into (for example your keyboard) may be able to read it.

4. Information we do not collect

  • No account, sign-up, email address or phone number.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. The App shows no ads.
  • No location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.
  • We never sell, share or look at your information.

5. Internet use

Your vault works fully offline. The App connects to the internet only for an update check: it downloads a small public version file from GitHub (karthic-keyan.github.io / raw.githubusercontent.com) and, if that fails, reads the App's public Google Play page. No vault data or personal information is sent. Like any web request, these servers can see your IP address. They are run by GitHub and Google, not by us. If you are offline, the check is skipped.

7. Backups, retention and deletion

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual items or folders inside the App.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for the App, so your vault is never copied to Google Drive or any other cloud service.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings (Apps > VaultGuard > Storage > Clear storage), permanently deletes the vault and its keys. Because we hold no copy, this cannot be undone.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
