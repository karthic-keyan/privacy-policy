Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 · ExpenseFlow (Expense Manager)

Key Commitment to Your Privacy

ExpenseFlow does NOT collect, upload, share, or sell any of your financial or personal information. The app is completely Ad-Free, runs 100% Offline, and stores all data exclusively on your device in an isolated local SQLite database.

1. Zero Remote Data Collection

ExpenseFlow does not transmit your records to any remote server or third-party service. You do not need to register an account, log in with an email address, or provide banking credentials to access all features of the application.

2. Local Information Processing

All data entered into the app remains strictly on your device:

  • Transactions: Amounts, dates, categories, notes, and types (expense/income).
  • Budgets & Limits: Monthly category-wise and overall limit parameters.
  • Subscriptions: Service names, billing frequencies, and cycle dates.
  • Investment Portfolio: Asset names, invested values, and current valuations.
  • App Preferences: Selected currency (e.g. INR, USD, EUR) and visual theme (System, Light, Dark).

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

Permission Usage & Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers scheduled local device reminders for bill renewals and budget warnings. No remote push notifications are used.
USE_BIOMETRIC / FINGERPRINT Facilitates biometric PIN/Fingerprint unlocking via Android BiometricPrompt hardware enclave. Raw biometric data is never accessed or stored.
STORAGE PERMISSIONS Used exclusively when you explicitly export transactions to CSV or choose a local backup file.

4. Advertising & Tracking Declarations

We declare that ExpenseFlow contains:

  • NO Advertisements: Zero banner ads, video ads, or interstitial advertisements.
  • NO Analytics SDKs: Zero tracking frameworks, third-party analytics libraries, or advertising identifiers.
  • NO Data Monetization: We never harvest, aggregate, or sell your financial habits.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your data as CSV files or completely delete all transactions and categories directly from the Settings screen. Uninstalling the application completely erases all local database records from your device.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at support@expenseflow.local.
