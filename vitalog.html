Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 • Vitalog (com.ktechinnovation.vitalog) • Published by ktechinnovation

Everything stays on your phone

Vitalog does not collect, upload, share or sell any of your information. The app is ad-free, works fully offline, needs no account, and stores your health data only on your device.

1. Information the app stores (on your device only)

  • Profiles: the name, avatar, colour, height, date of birth, gender, weight unit, start weight and goal weight you enter.
  • Health records: the weigh-ins, notes and body measurements (waist, chest, hips, body-fat percentage) you log.
  • Blood test reports: photos or PDF files of lab reports you add, the text read from them, and the test results you confirm or enter (for example hemoglobin, HbA1c or cholesterol values, reference ranges, report date and lab name).
  • Settings: theme, reminder times and profile lock settings. A PIN is stored only as a salted one-way hash, never in readable form.

This data is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Vitalog, so your health data is not copied to a cloud account either.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect personal or health information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, and the app shows no ads.
  • We do not collect location, contacts or device identifiers. The advertising ID permission is removed from the app.
  • The app has no account and no cloud sync.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we never receive any.

3. Fingerprint / biometrics

If you turn on fingerprint unlock for a profile, the app asks Android to verify your fingerprint. Vitalog never receives or stores fingerprint data; it only learns whether verification succeeded.

4. Camera and report reading

If you choose Take a photo, the app uses the camera only to photograph the report; the photo is saved in the app's private storage. Choose from gallery uses the Android photo picker, so the app only ever sees the photo you pick.

Text in report photos is read on your device with the text-recognition model built into Google ML Kit. Report images, the text read from them and your results are never uploaded to us, to Google or to anyone else. ML Kit includes an optional component that reports anonymous usage statistics to Google; Vitalog does not give it the permission it needs to send them, so nothing about your reports or your use of the app leaves your phone.

5. Notifications

Daily weigh-in reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved. After your phone restarts, the app re-schedules them on the device.

6. Internet use

All features work fully offline. The app makes one optional network request: an update check that downloads a small public version file from GitHub Pages to tell you when a newer version is on Google Play. No personal data is sent. If your device is offline, the check is simply skipped.

7. Backups and exports

When you choose Export backup or Export CSV, the app creates a file and opens the Android share sheet; you decide where it goes. A backup contains all profiles, including lab report photos and PDFs. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere private. Restore a backup (from Settings, or from the welcome screen on a new phone) reads a file you pick and replaces the app's data with it. We never receive these files.

8. Data deletion

You can delete individual entries, measurements, lab reports (including their photos and PDFs) or whole profiles within the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, permanently removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Medical disclaimer

Vitalog is for personal tracking only and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified health professional about your results.

10. Children's privacy

Vitalog does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
