Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Identity Vault (com.identityvault.app) • Published by k-Tech Innovation

Key Commitment to Your Privacy

Identity Vault does NOT collect, upload, share, or sell any of your personal information. The app is completely Ad-Free, runs 100% Offline, and stores all user data exclusively on your device.

1. Summary & Core Principles

Identity Vault is a hardware-encrypted on-device security vault for personal records.

  • Zero Remote Data Collection: We do not operate user analytics or external logging servers.
  • Local Processing: All application calculations and operations happen entirely within your local device CPU.
  • No Account Required: You never need to submit an email address, password, or profile information to use Identity Vault.

2. Information Handled Locally

Identity Vault encrypts user credentials, documents, and identity notes using AES-256 encryption. The encryption keys are securely protected by the Android KeyStore and your device hardware enclave.

3. Permissions Used

PermissionPurpose
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINTUnlocks your encrypted vault using hardware biometric authentication. Raw biometrics are never accessible to the app.
CAMERAUsed only when you explicitly take a photo of an ID document to store locally in your vault.

Advertising & Tracking Declarations

We strictly declare that Identity Vault contains:

  • NO Advertisements: Zero banner ads, video ads, or interstitial advertisements.
  • NO Analytics SDKs: Zero tracking frameworks, third-party analytics libraries, or advertising identifiers.
  • NO Data Monetization: We never harvest, aggregate, or sell your data.

Children's Privacy

Identity Vault does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for audiences of all ages.

Data Control & Deletion

You maintain full control of your data at all times. Since data is stored strictly on your device, clearing application storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data immediately.
