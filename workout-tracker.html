Works Offline • Your Workouts Stay on Your Device

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026 • Workout Tracker (com.ironlogtracker.app) • Published by k-Tech Innovation

Key Commitment to Your Privacy

Workout Tracker is a privacy-first gym app that works offline. We do NOT collect, upload, share, or sell your workout logs, personal records, profile, or body weight. They remain exclusively on your device. The app does not currently show ads; it includes Google's AdMob SDK so ads can be added in a future update (see Section 4).

1. Summary & Core Architecture

  • Your Workout Data Stays Local: We do not operate any servers, analytics or telemetry services.
  • Local Storage: All workout routines, sets, reps, and custom exercises are saved in local sandboxed storage on your device.
  • No Account Required: You never need to create an account, register with an email address, or provide phone numbers or passwords.
  • Advertising: No ads are shown at present. The Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) is included for possible future use.

2. Information Handled Strictly Locally

All processing occurs entirely on your device:

  • Workout Logs: Exercise names, muscle group categorizations, equipment types, personal notes, and session dates.
  • Set Metrics: Weights lifted (kg/lbs), target repetitions, completed sets, and volume tonnage calculations.
  • Personal Records (PRs): Historical milestone weights and reps stored locally for progressive overload tracking.
  • Templates & Custom Exercises: User-created split templates and custom movement definitions.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

Permission Type Explicit Purpose
VIBRATE Haptic Feedback Provides tactile confirmation when completing sets or adjusting steppers. No biometric data is accessed.
System Clipboard / Share User-Directed Action Used strictly when you tap "Share with AI" or "Copy Summary" to paste text into your notes or preferred AI tools. No background transmission occurs.
INTERNET Network Access Reserved for the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request ads if ads are added in a future update. Your workout data is never sent over the network.
AD_ID Advertising ID Lets the Google Mobile Ads SDK read your device's advertising ID for ads (see Section 4). You can reset or delete this ID in your device settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Workout Tracker includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC. The app does not currently request or show ads. If ads are added in a future update, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play listing first. When the app requests an ad, the SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address.
  • Usage and diagnostic information: such as ad views and taps, app launches, device model, operating system version, and SDK performance and crash data.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Your choices: You can reset your advertising ID, or delete it to opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads on some devices).
  • Your workouts are never shared: Workouts, personal records, profile, and body weight are never sent to Google or any ad network.
  • No Data Monetization: We never sell your data and use no analytics or data-broker services.

5. Google Play Data Safety Section Mapping

Workout Tracker declares the following in the Google Play Console Data safety form:

  • Data Collected & Shared: Device or other IDs (advertising ID), declared because the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the advertising ID permission are included in the app. Workout data is never collected.
  • Encrypted in Transit: Yes (the Google Mobile Ads SDK uses HTTPS).
  • Account Requirement: No account creation required.
  • Contains Ads: No, not at present. This will be changed to Yes before any ads are shown.

6. Data Control & Permanent Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: Delete individual workouts, sets, or templates directly inside the app.
  • Device Storage Clear: Permanently erase all local application data via Android Settings (Apps > Workouts > Storage > Clear Data).
  • App Uninstall: Completely uninstalling Workout Tracker removes all locally stored files immediately and irreversibly.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Workout Tracker is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Workout data entered in the app never leaves the device, whatever the user's age.

8. Security

Your workout records are never sent to any server. All files reside inside Android's sandboxed storage environment protected by OS-level file encryption.
