Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Workout Tracker (com.ironlogtracker.app) • Published by k-Tech Innovation

Key Commitment to Your Privacy

Workout Tracker is engineered from the ground up as a 100% Offline, Privacy-First, Ad-Free gym utility. We do NOT collect, upload, share, or sell any of your workout logs, personal information, or device data. All training routines, weights, repetitions, and personal records remain exclusively on your device.

1. Summary & Core Architecture

  • Zero Remote Data Collection: We do not operate external analytics or telemetry servers.
  • 100% Local Storage: All workout routines, sets, reps, and custom exercises are saved securely in local sandboxed storage on your device.
  • No Account Required: You never need to create an account, register with an email address, or provide phone numbers or passwords.
  • 100% Ad-Free: Zero banner ads, zero interstitial video popups, and zero sponsored tracker SDKs.

2. Information Handled Strictly Locally

All processing occurs entirely on your device:

  • Workout Logs: Exercise names, muscle group categorizations, equipment types, personal notes, and session dates.
  • Set Metrics: Weights lifted (kg/lbs), target repetitions, completed sets, and volume tonnage calculations.
  • Personal Records (PRs): Historical milestone weights and reps stored locally for progressive overload tracking.
  • Templates & Custom Exercises: User-created split templates and custom movement definitions.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

Permission Type Explicit Purpose
VIBRATE Haptic Feedback Provides tactile confirmation when completing sets or adjusting steppers. No biometric data is accessed.
System Clipboard / Share User-Directed Action Used strictly when you tap "Share with AI" or "Copy Summary" to paste text into your notes or preferred AI tools. No background transmission occurs.

4. Advertising & Tracking Declarations

In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, we declare that Workout Tracker contains:

  • NO Advertisements: Zero advertising SDKs (no Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network).
  • NO Analytics SDKs: Zero tracking frameworks, behavioral analytics, or third-party diagnostic brokers.
  • NO Data Monetization: We never harvest, aggregate, or sell your fitness records.

5. Google Play Data Safety Section Mapping

When reviewing Workout Tracker on Google Play, users and reviewers can verify that:

  • Data Collection: Does NOT collect user data.
  • Data Sharing: Does NOT share user data with third parties.
  • Account Requirement: No account creation required.
  • Ad-Supported: App is NOT ad-supported.

6. Data Control & Permanent Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: Delete individual workouts, sets, or templates directly inside the app.
  • Device Storage Clear: Permanently erase all local application data via Android Settings (Apps > Workouts > Storage > Clear Data).
  • App Uninstall: Completely uninstalling Workout Tracker removes all locally stored files immediately and irreversibly.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Workout Tracker collects zero personal information from any user, including children under 13. The application is completely safe for lifters of all ages.

8. Security

Your records remain safe because they never leave your device. All files reside inside Android's sandboxed storage environment protected by OS-level file encryption.
