# Privacy Policy for Clipboard History

**Effective Date: November 20, 2026** · Clipboard History (`com.clipboardnest.app`) · Published by k-Tech Innovation

Live copy: https://karthic-keyan.github.io/privacy-policy/clipboard-history.html (source: `privacy-policy.html` in this folder; keep both in sync)

> Clipboard History does **not** collect, upload, share, or sell any personal information. Your clipboard history is stored **only on your device**. No account, no cloud sync, no ads, no analytics.

## 1. Information stored on your device

- **Clipboard history**: While the App is open on screen and "Auto-save clipboard" is on, it reads the text on your clipboard and saves it to your local history. Clips you type or paste in manually are saved the same way.
- **Clip details**: text, time saved, pinned status, and detected type (link, email, phone, code…). Detection runs on-device.
- **Settings**: auto-save, history size, haptics, "Skip one-time codes".

All of this stays in the App's private storage and is never sent to us or anyone else.

## 2. Information we do NOT collect

- No personal information, no location, contacts, photos or device identifiers (the advertising ID permission is removed).
- No analytics, crash reporting, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- No account and no cloud sync.

## 3. Clipboard access

Android lets only the app on screen read the clipboard. Clipboard History reads it **only while it is open on your screen**, never in the background. On Android 13+ the system may show a notice when this happens. Clipboard contents can be sensitive, so you can turn off auto-save, enable "Skip one-time codes", and delete any clip or your whole history at any time.

## 4. Internet use

All features work offline. The only network use is an **update check**: the App downloads a public version file from `karthic-keyan.github.io` and, if that fails, reads the App's public Google Play page. No personal data or clipboard content is sent. GitHub/Google may log standard request details (e.g. IP address) under their own policies. Offline, the check is skipped.

## 5. Backups, sharing and other apps

- **Export backup** creates a JSON file and opens the share sheet; you choose where it goes.
- **Restore from backup** reads only the file you pick in the system file picker.
- **Share a clip** / **quick actions** (open link, email, phone) pass that text to the app you choose.
- **Send feedback** opens your email app with a pre-filled message; nothing is sent unless you send it.

Once data is handed to another app, that app's privacy policy applies.

## 6. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| INTERNET | Update check only |
| VIBRATE | Haptic feedback (can be turned off) |

No runtime ("dangerous") permissions. Storage, overlay, network-state and advertising-ID permissions are removed.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Clips stay until you delete them or they exceed your history limit (pinned clips are kept). Delete single clips, clear unpinned history, or delete everything in-app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes all data. We hold no copy of your data.

## 8. Security

Clips live in the App's private storage, which other apps can't read. Exported backups are unencrypted JSON, so store them somewhere you trust.

## 9. Children's privacy

Clipboard History collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Updates will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
