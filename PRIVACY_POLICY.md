# Privacy Policy for Motivate Daily

**Effective Date:** October 5, 2025  

Welcome to **Motivate Daily**. Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information related to your use of our Facebook Page.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from visitors of this Facebook Page directly.  
However, Facebook may collect information as described in [Facebook’s Data Policy](https://www.facebook.com/policy.php).

## 2. How We Use Information
Any engagement data (likes, comments, messages, etc.) received through Facebook is used only to:
- Respond to your messages or comments  
- Improve the quality of our posts and page engagement  

We do not use this information for advertising or data profiling outside Facebook.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.  
All data collected by Facebook is handled according to [Facebook’s own privacy terms](https://www.facebook.com/policy.php).

## 4. Security
We rely on Facebook’s platform security measures to protect data exchanged through this page.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected here with a new effective date.
