Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 • Simple Notes Offline (com.simplenotesoffline.app) • Published by ktechinnovation

Your notes stay on your phone

Simple Notes Offline does not collect, upload, share or sell any of your information. The app is ad-free, works fully offline, needs no account, and keeps your notes in an encrypted database on your device.

1. Information the app stores (on your device only)

  • Notes: titles, text (including Markdown), checklists, tags, colours, pinned / favourite / archived / trash status, reminders and timestamps.
  • Photos you attach: a copy of each photo you add to a note, kept in the app's private storage.
  • Settings: App Lock, fingerprint unlock, hidden previews and screenshot blocking. Your App Lock PIN is kept in Android Keystore-backed secure storage, never in a readable file.

The notes database is encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). Its key is generated on your device, kept in the Android Keystore, and never leaves the device. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your notes are not copied to a cloud account.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect notes, photos, search terms or any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, and the app shows no ads.
  • We do not collect location, contacts or device identifiers. The advertising ID permission is removed from the app.
  • The app has no account and no cloud sync.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we never receive any.

3. App Lock, locked notes and fingerprint

You can protect the app with a 4-digit PIN and lock individual notes. Locked notes are hidden in the notes list and search, and open only after your PIN or fingerprint. If you turn on fingerprint unlock, Android verifies your fingerprint; the app never receives or stores biometric data, only whether verification succeeded.

4. Photos

Photos are chosen with the Android system photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you pick. It does not request camera, storage or media-library permissions. A copy is kept in the app's private storage and deleted when you remove it from the note or delete the note permanently.

5. Reminders

Note reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved. After your phone restarts, the app re-schedules them on the device.

6. Internet use

All features work fully offline. The app makes one optional network request: an update check that downloads a small public version file from GitHub Pages (or, if that fails, reads the app's public Google Play page) to tell you when a newer version is available. No notes or personal data are sent. If your device is offline, the check is simply skipped.

7. Backups, exports and sharing

Backups are created only when you choose Export Backup. The app opens the Android share sheet and you decide where the file goes. You can protect a backup with a password: the file is then encrypted with AES-256, and the password is never stored. A backup without a password can be read by anyone who has the file. Photos are not included in backups. Restore from Backup reads a file you pick and merges it into your notes. Sharing a single note sends its text only to the app you choose. We never receive any of these files.

8. Data deletion

You can delete notes, empty the trash, or remove tags within the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently erases the encrypted database, its key and all attached photos from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children's privacy

Simple Notes Offline does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
