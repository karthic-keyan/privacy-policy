Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • ClipNest (com.clipboardnest.app) • Published by k-Tech Innovation

Key Commitment to Your Privacy

ClipNest does NOT collect, upload, share, or sell any of your personal information. Your clipboard history is stored only on your device. The app has no account, no cloud sync, no ads, and no analytics.

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipNest ("the App"), developed by k-Tech Innovation ("we", "us"), handles your information.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Clipboard history: When the App is open on your screen and "Auto-save clipboard" is turned on, it reads the text currently on your clipboard and saves it to your local history. Clips you type or paste in manually are saved the same way.
  • Clip details: For each clip the App stores the text, when it was saved, whether it is pinned, and its detected type (for example, link, email, phone number or code). Type detection runs entirely on your device.
  • Settings: Your preferences, such as auto-save, history size, haptic feedback, and "Skip one-time codes".

All of this data is kept in the App's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, or account details).
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. The advertising ID permission is explicitly removed from the App.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No account sign-up and no cloud sync.

3. Clipboard Access

On Android, only the app that is currently on screen can read the clipboard. ClipNest reads the clipboard only while it is open on your screen, and only to save the text to your local history. It does not run in the background and does not watch your clipboard while it is closed. On Android 13 and later, the system may show a short notice when the App reads the clipboard.

Clipboard contents may include sensitive information such as passwords or one-time codes. You control what is stored:

  • Turn off Settings → Auto-save clipboard to stop the App from reading the clipboard automatically.
  • Turn on Skip one-time codes to avoid saving short numeric verification codes.
  • Delete any single clip, clear unpinned history, or delete all clips at any time.

4. Internet Use

All features work fully offline. The App connects to the internet only to check for updates. It downloads a small public version file from our GitHub Pages site (karthic-keyan.github.io) and, if that file can't be reached, reads the App's public Google Play listing page. These requests send no personal data or clipboard content. As with any web request, the servers receiving it (GitHub or Google) may log standard technical details such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. If your device is offline, the check is skipped.

5. Backups, Sharing & Other Apps

  • Export backup: Creates a backup file of your clips and opens the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example, a file manager or cloud drive). We never receive it.
  • Restore from backup: Opens the system file picker so you can choose a backup file. The App reads only the file you select.
  • Share a clip: Sends that clip's text to the app you choose.
  • Quick actions: Opening a link, email address, or phone number from a clip passes it to your browser, email, or dialer app.
  • Send feedback: Opens your email app with a pre-filled message to us. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

Once data is handed to another app or service you selected, that app's own privacy policy applies.

6. Permissions Used

PermissionPurpose
INTERNETUsed only for the optional update check described above.
VIBRATEHaptic feedback when you copy, pin, or delete clips. You can turn this off in Settings.

ClipNest does not request any runtime ("dangerous") permissions. Storage, overlay, network-state, and advertising-ID permissions are explicitly removed.

Advertising & Tracking Declarations

ClipNest contains:

  • NO Advertisements: No banner, video, or interstitial ads.
  • NO Analytics SDKs: No tracking frameworks, third-party analytics libraries, or advertising identifiers.
  • NO Data Monetization: We never collect, aggregate, or sell your data.

Data Retention & Deletion

Clips stay on your device until you delete them or until they are removed automatically when your history exceeds the size limit you set (pinned clips are kept). You can delete individual clips, clear unpinned history, or delete all clips from within the App. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App permanently removes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive your data, we have nothing stored on our side to delete.

Security

Your clips are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps on your device cannot read. Exported backup files are plain JSON and are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Children's Privacy

ClipNest does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
